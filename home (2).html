Common questions.

If your question isn't answered here, just email contactambitionedge@gmail.com and you'll hear back from me personally.

Is one session really enough to get real value?
Yes. The Audit phase is built to give you clarity, priorities, and a real written plan within 90 minutes. Many clients put major changes into action that same week. If a deeper engagement makes sense, we'll talk about it. If not, you still leave with a clear roadmap you can use.
How is this different from "business coaching"?
Coaching mostly asks you questions and leaves the answers to you. Consulting brings you clear diagnoses, frameworks, and decisions. Ambition Edge delivers structured, evidence-based consulting through the A.R.I.F. Method™, with the warmth and personal accountability you'd expect from a private practice rather than a faceless firm.
Do you work outside Calgary?
Yes. Around half of Ambition Edge engagements are with clients outside Calgary. Calgary clients have the option of meeting in person, and everyone else receives the same depth and quality of partnership remotely.
What's the guarantee?
If you leave the diagnostic without at least one clear, valuable insight you can act on right away, the session is refunded. Our standard is simple: every client should be better off starting from the very first session.
How quickly can we meet?
Most sessions are booked within 5 to 10 business days of your application. There are a limited number of slots each month, so it's worth applying early. You'll receive a calendar link within one business day.
